A significant debate centers on the technical capabilities and limitations of current AI systems. [Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2109551004635332815) emphasizes that generative AI cannot reliably follow simple instructions—a critical cognitive deficit relative to humans. He argues that conflating older "stochastic parrot" LLMs with modern neurosymbolic systems that integrate symbolic verification, code interpreters, and formal reasoning misrepresents current technological progress and dismisses the importance of symbolic AI contributions.
