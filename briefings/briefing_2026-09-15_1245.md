## Theme 4: Targeted AI Deployment Pauses vs. Blanket Restrictions

[Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2099667346453663783) clarifies his position: he advocates a temporary pause on *specific* AI technologies (general-purpose agents with internet access) proven unreliable, not all AI development.
