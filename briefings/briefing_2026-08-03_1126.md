# Executive Summary

The European Union has begun enforcing its AI Act rules, requiring mandatory disclosure when citizens interact with AI systems or encounter AI-generated or AI-edited content. According to reporting from Wired, this regulatory approach is generating concern among stakeholders about "disclosure fatigue"—the risk that ubiquitous AI disclosures will become so routine that citizens cease to meaningfully process them, thereby undermining the transparency objective itself. The enforcement marks a significant shift toward prescriptive AI governance in Europe, though the practical effectiveness of disclosure-based regulation remains uncertain if compliance becomes merely procedural rather than genuinely informative.
