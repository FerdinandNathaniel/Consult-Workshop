# Executive Summary

The European Union's AI Act transparency requirements, which entered force on August 2nd, now mandate that companies disclose when users interact with AI systems and identify AI-generated content such as deepfakes. This regulatory development represents a significant shift toward algorithmic accountability in the bloc's digital economy, establishing enforceable disclosure obligations that will likely influence compliance standards across major technology platforms operating in EU markets. The timing and scope of these rules suggest an emerging pattern of EU regulatory leadership in AI governance, potentially creating compliance pressures that extend beyond European jurisdictions as multinational firms standardize their disclosure practices globally.
